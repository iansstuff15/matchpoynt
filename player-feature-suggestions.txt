Here are ideas for the player side that aren't built yet. Each is a short line; the bold ones are the ones I'd do first.

**On the night**
1. **"You're up next" notifications**: a web push or email when you're one match from court, or your court opens.
2. **Self check-in from Playing now**: check in when you arrive (or by geofence) instead of scanning the QR.
3. **Pause / break**: mark yourself "on a break" so the queue skips you for a while, decided by the API.
4. **Preferred partners and avoid list**: soft requests the matchmaking may honour.
5. **Score confirmation**: both sides confirm or dispute a result from their phones.
6. **Live court view for spectators**: a public board for family and friends watching.

**Your record**
7. **Head-to-head**: your record against or with a given player, and your best partners and toughest opponents.
8. **Form and trends**: win rate over time, last 10 matches, longest streak, points per game, per sport.
9. **Personal bests and milestones**: 100th match, longest rally win, first tournament win, shown as cards you can share.
10. **Season or year in review**: a shareable recap ("your 2026 on court").
11. **Rankings across clubs**: a per-sport rating (Elo-style) that moves with results, shown on your profile.

**Badges and collection**
12. **Badges still to earn**: progress bars toward each club's badges ("2 of 3 wins").
13. **Showcase**: pin your favourite badges to your profile header.
14. **Rarity**: how many players hold each badge.

**Social**
15. **Friends' activity feed**: who played, who earned what, friends' sessions tonight.
16. **Invite friends to a session**: ask your friends to come to a club's planned session.
17. **Public profile page** (`/u/<username>`): record, badges and clubs, with privacy settings.
18. **Kudos or reactions** on a friend's results or badges.
19. **Chat or announcements** per club or session (club owner to members).

**Planning and money**
20. **Calendar of upcoming sessions** across your clubs, with RSVP ("I'm coming") and .ics export.
21. **Your invoices**: what you owe across clubs, with payment links or a "mark as sent" option.
22. **Waitlist for full sessions**, with automatic promotion.
23. **Availability**: tell your clubs which nights you can usually play.

**Account and comfort**
24. **Notification settings** for which emails and pushes you get.
25. **Install as an app (PWA)**, with home-screen badges for "you're up".
26. **Privacy controls**: hide your record or badges from non-friends.
27. **Multi-sport tier self-assessment**, plus a request for a club to verify your tier.

My top five for impact are **#1, #20, #7, #12 and #21**. They cover the biggest player pain points: knowing when to play, knowing when sessions are, rivalry, progress, and paying.
